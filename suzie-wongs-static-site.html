Our Story

So Who The
F*ck Is
Suzie Wong?

Nobody knows. And that's part of the magic.

Suzie Wong's Good Time Bar is Fortitude Valley's worst kept secret - a Hawaiian-inspired dive bar and live music venue that's been providing Brisbane with exactly the kind of nights you can't quite explain to someone who wasn't there.

We're accessed through a retro fridge door. There are surfboards hanging from the ceiling. A Jameson neon sign keeps watch over the bar. The walls are covered in frames. The music is always too loud.

We are not a cocktail bar. We are not a restaurant. We are a dive bar with a world-class stage, a staff that genuinely gives a shit, and a crowd that knows how to have a good time.

"Life is too short
to drink responsibly."
